Department of Commerce

Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi

The Department of Commerce prepares students for careers in accounting, finance, marketing and business management. Through classroom teaching, seminars, workshops and student-led societies, we build both subject knowledge and practical skills.

Explore Courses Contact Us

About the Department

The department offers the B.Com Programme, which combines core commerce subjects with skill-based and elective papers. Students learn accounting, business law, finance, marketing and management, and apply them through presentations, case studies and society events.

Vision

To develop ethical, skilled and confident commerce graduates ready for industry and higher studies.

Mission

  • Strong conceptual teaching
  • Practical, skill-based learning
  • Industry and community exposure

Highlights

  • Experienced faculty
  • Regular seminars and workshops
  • Active student societies
